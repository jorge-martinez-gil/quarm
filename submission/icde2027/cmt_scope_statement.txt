QuaRM is a regular-research contribution at the intersection of data quality, analytical query processing, and experimental data systems. It introduces a counterfactual mechanism-coalition risk surface for SQL and ML workloads; derives exact Shapley accounting, interaction diagnostics, full-context intervention gains, and a sampled estimator; and implements the design in a deterministic DuckDB/Python system. The evaluation uses an official NYC TLC snapshot, controlled star schemas, eight SQL queries, ML pipelines, exact factorial studies, uncertainty analysis, and million-row scaling. The work directly addresses ICDE topics in data preparation/cleaning/integration, data quality/curation/provenance/workflows, and benchmarking/testing.
